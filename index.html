I'm a 22-year-old full-stack web developer from Egypt, currently studying at Borg El Arab Technological University. Most of my time goes into building complete web products — React/Next.js front ends backed by Node/NestJS APIs and real databases — end to end, from UI to deployment.

Most of what's below started as a class project or something I got curious about and kept pushing on: a graduation project with 3D drug models, a QR generator I still tweak on weekends, a produce delivery storefront I'm building for a real local store.
